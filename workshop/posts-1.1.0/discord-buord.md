## Rats Pop-Out Windows 1.1 is out
The pop-out mod grew up. Windows stay where you put them, snap, maximize and can go see-through, and the Encyclopedia got a proper search. Most of it works on one monitor too:
- **Remembered windows**: place and size come back, also after a restart
- **Double-click** a title bar to maximize, windows **snap** to edges and each other
- **See-through windows**: opacity, scale and level per window
- **Encyclopedia search** in ids and descriptions, readable loadout names, Armed only
- **Unit Status Reference tab**, draggable time panel, right-click menu on the unit panel
- **Second monitor** as before: one click pops a window out

**Game version** For 0.8.7, the globe beta comes later.
Needs **Anchor Chain**.
